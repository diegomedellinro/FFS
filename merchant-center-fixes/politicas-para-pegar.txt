30-day guarantee
You have 30 days from the day you receive your order to request an exchange, a return or a refund. This applies to all jerseys, including jerseys bought on sale or with a discount code.

Conditions
- The jersey must be unworn, unwashed, with its tags and in its original packaging.
- Jerseys with a custom name and number (personalized) can be exchanged or refunded only if there is a printing error or a defect.
- You will need your order number or proof of purchase.

How to request an exchange or return
1. Write to us on WhatsApp at +52 55 1294 1852 or by email at diegomedellinro@gmail.com, within 30 days of receiving your order.
2. Send your order number and a photo of the jersey (and of the problem, if there is one).
3. We confirm your request and send you the instructions and the address to send the jersey back. Items sent back without first contacting us cannot be accepted.

Return address

Return shipping
- If the jersey arrives damaged, with a defect, or you received the wrong item, we pay the return shipping and send you the replacement for free.
- If you want to change the size or return a jersey for another reason, the return shipping is paid by the customer.

Exchanges
The fastest way to get the right size is an exchange. Once we receive the jersey, we send you the new size with free shipping.

Refunds
When we receive and inspect the jersey, we will tell you if your refund is approved. Approved refunds are made to your original payment method within 10 business days. Damaged or wrong items
Please check your order when you receive it. If the jersey is damaged, has a defect or is not what you ordered, write to us as soon as possible so we can fix it.
